The problem

Travel shouldn't feel like charades.

Passing a phone back and forth kills the conversation. Translation apps show text to only one person, and nobody remembers what was said afterwards.

The big idea

Two displays. One conversation.

Stand the Duo up like a tiny laptop on the café table. The inner display faces you with the full conversation. The outer display faces them with big, live captions in their language, flipped the right way up.
